# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro (static output, zero client framework), plain CSS and a small amount of vanilla JavaScript, hosted free on Cloudflare Pages from GitHub. Chosen so the menu and footer are written once and shared by every page, while visitors still receive plain, fast HTML. No paid tools or services.

## Users

Owners and managers of local small businesses in Moncton, New Brunswick: restaurants, shops, trades, salons and clinics. Most arrive from the studio's Instagram on a phone, often between jobs or after hours. They want to know, quickly, what the studio makes, whether it suits a business like theirs, and how to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Atlantic & Co. Media is a small media and marketing studio in Moncton. It makes promotional reels, runs photo and video shoots, plans social media strategy, and designs and builds websites for local businesses. The website exists to make a visitor feel the studio's standard of work within seconds and to turn interest into a message (Instagram DM or email). Success is a local owner reaching out.

## Positioning

A local, founder-led studio that gives Moncton small businesses the cinematic, premium look usually reserved for big brands, made by someone in the same city who can come to the shop, the kitchen or the job site.

## Operating Context

- Discovery happens on Instagram (@atlantic.co.media, https://www.instagram.com/atlantic.co.media); the site is the next tap.
- Conversations start by Instagram message or email; there is no booking system and no online payment.
- The studio is new: "Something new is coming to Moncton."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: promotional reels; photo and video shoots; social media marketing strategy; web design and creation.
- No prices are shown anywhere on the site.
- Contact: Instagram is live. A public email address does not exist yet (open decision); the email link is added once it does. A contact form is added only if it truly delivers to the inbox, with Cloudflare Turnstile spam protection.
- No domain yet (open decision); the site runs on a free Cloudflare Pages address until one is chosen.
- Headlines and intro copy name Moncton only; a service-area line may mention surrounding New Brunswick.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Atlantic & Co. Media. Monogram "A&Co." in gold inside a double circle with waves; wordmark "Atlantic & Co." with "MEDIA" in gold, on black.
- Voice: confident, warm, plain-spoken, local. Canadian spelling (colour, centre, favourite).
- Visual direction pinned by the founder: "Midnight Gold" (see DESIGN.md).

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo files: `public/brand/` (monogram, supplied as JPEG on black). The supplied wordmark file is white on white and cannot be used on the dark site; the wordmark is set in type until a transparent original is supplied.
- No client work, photographs or footage of the studio's own yet. Stock media (licences allowing commercial use) stands in for atmosphere only and is never presented as the studio's own client work. Work tiles stay clearly labelled placeholders until real work is added.
- There are no testimonials, awards, statistics, prices, client names or street address. None may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show the standard, don't claim it: the page itself must look like the first frame of a good reel.
2. One clear next step everywhere: message us.
3. Local and personal over corporate: Moncton, the founder, real conversations.
4. Honest by default: placeholders are labelled; nothing is invented.
5. Phone first: built for a thumb arriving from Instagram.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, descriptive alt text, captions or muted decorative video, and full respect for prefers-reduced-motion.
